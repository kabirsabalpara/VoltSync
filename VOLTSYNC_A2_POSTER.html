Smart EV Charging Station Finder & Concurrency-Safe Slot Booking System

Abstract Summary

In contemporary urban mobility networks, decentralized Electric Vehicle (EV) drivers suffer severe charging friction due to fragmented station directories, unverified stall occupancy, and double-booking collisions during peak rush hours.

VoltSync introduces an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant digital charging station locator and slot reservation ecosystem connecting independent Charge Point Operators (CPOs) across Surat and Gujarat under centralized administrative telemetry oversight.

The system automates geospatial connector discovery (CCS-2, CHAdeMO, Type-2), evaluates vehicle-specific battery State-of-Charge (SoC) curves, enforces an atomic 5-minute temporary reservation lock, and issues digital scannable QR FastPass check-in tickets (100% collision prevention).

Introduction & Objectives Problem & Aims

Problem Statement: Fragmented charging stations operate in informational silos without synchronized slot reservation, causing severe "range anxiety", physical queue bottlenecks, and high idle capacity overhead.

Key Project Objectives:

  • Interactive Geospatial Mapping: Leaflet & OpenStreetMap integration with live status badges and corridor radius query.
  • Atomic 5-Minute Slot Hold: Database-level concurrency lock preventing double-booking during checkout.
  • Smart EV Battery Estimator: Precise model-based charging duration and tariff prediction (Tata Nexon, MG ZS, Ioniq 5).
  • Digital FastPass Verification: Instant scannable QR check-in ticket with unique alphanumeric booking code.
  • Operator Command Telemetry: RBAC dashboard for station onboarding, live stall toggle, and revenue auditing.
Outcome: 100% of defined objectives achieved with 25 passed automated regression test suites and verified staging deployment.
